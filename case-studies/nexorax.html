FLAGSHIP // 01 · CASE STUDY

NexoraX

A security-first intelligence and operations platform designed for authorized investigations, evidence, relationships, timelines and grounded AI-assisted analysis.

10-phase V1Next.js + FastAPIPostgreSQL + RLSOIDC / JWT
THE SYSTEM

Analytical power without bypassing authorization.

NexoraX is structured around a simple rule: sensitive intelligence should only become visible, searchable or available to AI after identity, organization membership, permissions, clearance and database row policies have been enforced.

ARCHITECTURE
Identity→Tenant / RBAC / Clearance→Entities & Cases→Evidence→Secure Search→Graph→Grounded AI
CORE CAPABILITIES

Evidence integrity

Custody-aware evidence handling with integrity-oriented workflows.

Secure search

Authorization-aware discovery designed so inaccessible records do not enter results.

Relationship graph

Entity linking and correlation for analytical leads without treating correlation as proof.

Grounded AI

AI assistance constrained to authorized context with source citations and human review.

Watchlists & alerts

Deterministic exact-reference monitoring rather than predictive scoring.

Responsible use

Explicit boundaries against predictive policing, guilt scoring, biometric identification and automated coercive decisions.

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES
  • Server-derived identity and tenant context
  • Forced PostgreSQL row-level security
  • Clearance-aware access control
  • Human-reviewed AI assistance
  • Evidence treated differently from AI output
